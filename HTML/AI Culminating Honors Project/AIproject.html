Honors Culminating Project
Topic: AI in a postmodern world.

Title: Learning machines and forgotten identities.

Central Question: As AI reshapes how we understand, create, and connect, what identities does it reinforce or replicate?

Reasoning: This question matters because my research suggests that AI is not neutral since it is not an autonomous consciousness and relies on algorithms; it learns from existing cultural biases, dominant narratives, and systemic exclusions. By examining AI through a postmodern lens, we can uncover the discriminatory representations behind seemingly objective technologies.

Research Statement: This project investigates the role of AI in shaping identities by influencing cultural narratives and knowledge systems about identity. Using a postmodern lens, it explores how AI both reflects and reinforces dominant ideologies while potentially erasing marginalized voices and ways of knowing. Through a website, the project collects human perspectives, blends theoretical critique, and creates a fragmented, multimedia reflection on what AI reveals and conceals about identities. The website will function as an interactive archive, art piece, or critical commentary platform inviting visitors to explore how AI interacts and represents identity in today’s world.
